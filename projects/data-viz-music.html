Music & Data Visualization

Data SciencePythonDesign

An exploration of how listening habits and musical patterns can be visualized through interactive data graphics.

This project explores the intersection of music and data — two of my biggest interests. Using Python and listening history data, I built visualizations that reveal patterns in genre, mood, tempo, and listening time across different periods.

The goal was to make personal data feel personal: instead of generic bar charts, the visuals use musical metaphors like waveforms and rhythm grids to represent the data in a way that feels connected to the subject matter.
